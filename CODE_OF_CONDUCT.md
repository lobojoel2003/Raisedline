# Code of conduct

Raisedline follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, and remember that many people here
are blind or have low vision. Describe images and screenshots in issues and pull requests,
and don't rely on colour alone to make a point.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be
handled confidentially.
